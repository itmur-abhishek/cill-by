# Original Chil by Penguin image integration

The supplied original product and brand brochure images are stored in `src/assets/`:

- `chil-blueberry-original.jpeg` — used in the homepage hero, product catalogue, and product detail page. The image is displayed with `object-fit: contain` to preserve the complete original packaging and artwork.
- `chil-brand-brochure.jpeg` — displayed in the homepage brand identity section as a visual brand reference.

The existing lifestyle image remains in the Our Story section. No additional product flavours or prices have been inferred from the brochure.
